{% include 'blocks/_middle_heading.html' with title="Medical and Emergency Treatment" %}

The Club takes reasonable steps to support the health and safety of players participating in Club activities and to ensure that appropriate action can be taken in the event of illness or injury.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Medical Information" %}

Players are encouraged to tell the Club about any medical condition which may be relevant to their safe participation in hockey, such as asthma, allergies or other conditions which a coach, captain or team official may need to be aware of.

Medical information will be handled in accordance with the Club's Data and Privacy Policy and will only be made available to those who have an appropriate reason to know it.

Players are responsible for ensuring that the information they provide is accurate and kept up to date.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Emergency Contacts" %}

Players should provide an appropriate emergency contact and ensure that their contact details are kept up to date.

In the event of a serious illness or injury, the Club will make reasonable efforts to contact the player's emergency contact as soon as practicable.

The need to obtain urgent medical assistance will always take priority over contacting an emergency contact.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Emergency Medical Treatment" %}

Where a player becomes ill or injured during a Club activity, Club officials will take reasonable steps to obtain appropriate first aid or medical assistance.

Where immediate medical attention is required, the Club will contact the emergency services or otherwise arrange appropriate medical assistance without unnecessary delay.

The Club will not delay necessary emergency treatment while attempting to contact an emergency contact.

Where relevant, information known to the Club about the player's medical conditions, medication, allergies or other matters affecting their treatment may be provided to healthcare professionals or emergency services.

Clinical decisions about treatment will be made by the healthcare professionals responsible for the player's care.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Consent" %}

As part of registration, players are asked to consent to necessary emergency medical treatment being provided if they become incapacitated and are unable to make decisions for themselves.

For players under 18, appropriate consent will be obtained from a parent or carer.

Emergency treatment will not be withheld or delayed solely because consent cannot be confirmed where healthcare professionals determine that immediate treatment is necessary.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Medication" %}

Players who require medication during hockey activities, such as inhalers or adrenaline auto-injectors, should ensure that it is available when required and should make an appropriate coach, captain or team official aware of its location where necessary.

Club officials will not normally administer medication unless this is appropriate in an emergency or specific arrangements have been agreed in advance.
