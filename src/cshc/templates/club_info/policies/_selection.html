{% include 'blocks/_middle_heading.html' with title="Selection" %}

We aim to provide enjoyable, competitive hockey for players of all abilities, while enabling every team to compete at the highest appropriate level and giving players opportunities to develop and progress.

Men's and Ladies' teams are selected each week by the relevant captains and coaches, working together across the section. Teams are selected in hierarchical order, starting with the 1st XI and working down through the teams.

Selection is based on a combination of factors, including:

  • eligibility to play
  • playing ability, form and fitness
  • positional and tactical requirements and overall team balance
  • performance and attendance at training
  • availability, reliability and commitment
  • attitude, conduct and contribution to the team
  • player development and potential

No single factor determines selection, and the relative importance of these factors may vary between teams and during the season.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Player Movement" %}

Players should expect to move between teams during the season as availability, form and the needs of the Club change. Promotion and relegation between teams is a normal part of selection and should not be regarded as permanent.

Where practical, players moving to a lower team should be told personally by their captain or coach and given an explanation of the decision.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Availability" %}

Players are responsible for keeping their availability up to date and should provide as much notice as possible of any changes. Once teams have been selected, late changes to availability may affect where a player can be accommodated.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Fairness and Development" %}

Selection decisions will be made fairly and in the best interests of the Club as a whole. We aim to balance competitive performance with player development, participation and the long-term strength of all our teams.

All selections must comply with the current England Hockey and East Area League regulations and guidance.
