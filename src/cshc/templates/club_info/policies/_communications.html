{% include 'blocks/_middle_heading.html' with title="Communications" %}

The Club uses communication to keep members informed, organise hockey and other activities, and support an effective and welcoming Club environment.

We aim to communicate clearly, appropriately and through channels which are accessible to the people who need the information.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Official Club Communication" %}

Spond is the Club's primary platform for official communication relating to membership, fixtures, training, availability, team activities and other Club matters.

The Club may also use email, the Club website and other official channels where appropriate.

Members are responsible for ensuring that the Club has accurate and up-to-date contact details and for checking the communication channels relevant to them.

Official Club communications should be used for Club purposes and should be appropriate, respectful and relevant to the intended audience.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Team Communication" %}

Teams may use informal communication channels, including WhatsApp groups, to supplement official Club communication.

These groups are not a replacement for Spond or other official Club communications. Important information should also be provided through the official Club communication channel where appropriate, so that members are not disadvantaged because they do not use a particular messaging app.

Team communication groups should be used primarily for hockey-related matters and should be administered responsibly. Where under-18 players are included, the requirements in the section below apply.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Communication with Under-18s" %}

The Club follows England Hockey's safeguarding guidance when communicating with players under 18.

Communication with under-18 players by Club officers, coaches, captains, managers and other adults acting on behalf of the Club should:

  • be necessary and related to Club or hockey activities
  • use an appropriate Club or team communication channel
  • where individual written communication with an under-18 is necessary, include a parent or carer or another appropriate responsible adult
  • allow the young person an appropriate opportunity to communicate and raise questions or concerns
  • use language and content which is appropriate for their age and circumstances

Club representatives should not use personal social-media accounts to establish private relationships with under-18 players.

One-to-one communication with an under-18 player should be avoided wherever reasonably possible. Where individual communication is necessary, it should be transparent and, where appropriate, include a parent or carer or another responsible adult.

For urgent changes to arrangements, teams should use a suitable communication chain or other method which ensures that both the young person and their parent or carer receive the information.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="WhatsApp and Similar Messaging Groups" %}

Where a team uses WhatsApp or another group messaging service:

  • the group should have at least two responsible adults as members
  • parents or carers should be informed about the group's purpose and how it will be used
  • the group should be used primarily for hockey-related communication
  • group members should not be expected to use the group as their only source of important Club information
  • private one-to-one messaging between adults acting on behalf of the Club and under-18 players should not be used for routine Club communication
  • selection decisions, performance feedback and other sensitive matters should not be discussed in group messaging channels

These principles apply equally to any similar messaging or communication platform.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Parent and Carer Communication" %}

For junior members, parents and carers will normally be included in communications concerning Club activities.

Parents and carers should ensure that the Club has an appropriate contact number and email address and should tell the Club if they wish to be included directly in communications with their child.

The Club will use the contact details provided for Club-related purposes and will handle them in accordance with the Club's Data and Privacy Policy.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Concerns and Inappropriate Communication" %}

Members should report inappropriate, offensive or concerning communications to a Club officer or the Club's Welfare Officer.

Any safeguarding concern involving communication with a young person should be reported in accordance with the Club's safeguarding procedures and England Hockey guidance.
