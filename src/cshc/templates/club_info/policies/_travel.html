Cambridge South Hockey Club teams regularly travel to away matches, tournaments and other Club activities. The Club encourages members to share transport where practical and aims to ensure that travel arrangements involving players under 18 follow England Hockey's safeguarding guidance.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Travel to Matches and Club Activities" %}

Players are normally responsible for getting themselves to and from matches, training and other Club activities.

For senior teams, the Club facilitates lift-sharing between players, primarily through Spond. Captains and players may use Spond to identify who can drive, who requires a lift and to coordinate suitable travel arrangements.

Players are not required to provide lifts and remain responsible for making appropriate travel arrangements.

Drivers providing lifts in their own vehicles are responsible for ensuring that:

  • they are legally entitled and fit to drive
  • their vehicle is roadworthy and appropriately insured
  • the number of passengers carried is appropriate for the vehicle
  • passengers use seat belts and any other restraints required by law
{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Players Under 18" %}

Anyone under the age of 18 is considered a young person for safeguarding purposes.

A parent or carer remains responsible for agreeing appropriate travel arrangements for their child.

An under-18 player may travel in another person's private vehicle only where their parent or carer has given explicit consent to the particular travel arrangement.

Before giving consent, the parent or carer should know who will be providing the transport and have sufficient information about the journey and arrangements.

Where lift-sharing is coordinated through Spond or another Club communication channel, arrangements involving an under-18 player must therefore be agreed with their parent or carer and not solely with the player.

General acknowledgement of this Travel Policy or participation in Club activities does not constitute consent to an individual journey.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Adults Transporting under-18 Players" %}

Adults providing transport to under-18 players must act responsibly and in accordance with the Club's Safeguarding Policy and England Hockey's SafeGuard guidance.

Wherever reasonably possible, there should be more than one adult travelling in a vehicle carrying under-18 players.

Where an adult will be travelling alone with an unrelated under-18 player, this must be made clear to the parent or carer when their consent is obtained. The journey should be directly related to the relevant Club activity and the arrangements should be open and transparent.

Adults should not make private or undisclosed travel arrangements directly with under-18 players for Club activities.

England Hockey provides specific safeguarding guidance for adults on teams with young players, which should be followed by adult members of senior teams containing under-18 players.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Changes to Travel Arrangements" %}

Travel arrangements sometimes need to change at short notice.

Where a change affects an under-18 player, reasonable efforts must be made to inform their parent or carer and obtain their agreement before alternative transport is arranged.

The welfare of the young person must remain the priority. An under-18 player should not be left without appropriate supervision or a safe means of getting home because planned transport arrangements have changed.

Where an unforeseen situation requires immediate action to safeguard a young person, those responsible should act in the young person's best interests and inform their parent or carer as soon as reasonably practicable.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Club-Organised Transport" %}

Occasionally, the Club may specifically organise group transport, for example by hiring a coach or minibus.

Where the Club organises transport, appropriate arrangements will be made for the safety and supervision of those travelling.

Parents or carers of under-18 players will be informed of the arrangements and appropriate consent will be obtained. {% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Safeguarding" %}

All travel arrangements involving under-18 players must comply with the Club's Safeguarding Policy and England Hockey's SafeGuard policies and Good Practice guidance.

Any safeguarding concern arising from travel involving a young person should be reported to the Club Welfare Officer in accordance with the Club's safeguarding procedures.
