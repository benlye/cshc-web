Cambridge South Hockey Club is committed to providing a safe and positive environment for all young people involved in hockey.

The Club has adopted England Hockey's Safeguarding Policy, Reporting Procedures and Good Practice Guidance. All Club members, coaches, officials and volunteers are expected to follow these policies whenever they are responsible for, or participating in hockey with, young people.

Any concern about the welfare of a young person, or about the behaviour of an adult towards a young person, should be reported to the Club's Welfare Officer in accordance with England Hockey's safeguarding procedures.
