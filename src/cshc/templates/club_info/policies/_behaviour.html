Cambridge South Hockey Club aims to provide a friendly, welcoming, inclusive and enjoyable environment for everyone involved in the Club.

Everyone involved in the Club is expected to behave in a way that reflects these values and to treat teammates, opponents, umpires, coaches, officials, volunteers and spectators with respect.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Standards of Behaviour" %}

All members are expected to:

  • behave in a way that reflects the Club's values and principles
  • follow the Club's Code of Conduct and its Think SOUTH principles
  • abide by England Hockey's Spirit of Hockey – Code of Conduct
  • respect the decisions of umpires, coaches and other officials
  • behave responsibly and respectfully both on and off the pitch
  • help create an environment in which everyone feels welcome, included and safe
  • avoid abusive, intimidating, discriminatory, harassing or otherwise inappropriate behaviour
  • behave in a way which does not bring the Club or the sport into disrepute.

These expectations apply whenever a member is representing or participating in the activities of the Club, including matches, training, tournaments, social events and Club-related communications and online activity.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Everyone's Responsibility" %}

Maintaining the culture of the Club is everyone's responsibility.

Members are encouraged to challenge inappropriate behaviour where it is safe and appropriate to do so, and to raise concerns with a captain, coach, Club Captain, Welfare Officer or committee member.

Concerns involving the welfare or safeguarding of a child or young person should be reported in accordance with the Club's safeguarding procedures. {% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Breaches of the Code of Conduct" %}

Behaviour which falls below the standards expected by the Club will be dealt with in accordance with the Club's Code of Conduct.

Depending on the circumstances, the Club may address an issue informally or take formal action. Serious or repeated breaches may result in sanctions including suspension from Club activities or termination of membership.

Conduct which may breach England Hockey's Spirit of Hockey – Code of Conduct or other England Hockey regulations may also be reported and dealt with through the appropriate England Hockey disciplinary process.

Members are expected to cooperate with any reasonable investigation or disciplinary process arising from a concern about behaviour.
