Cambridge South Hockey Club is committed to making hockey welcoming, accessible and enjoyable for everyone.

We support England Hockey's commitment to equality, diversity and inclusion and believe that everyone involved in the Club should be treated fairly, with dignity and respect.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Our Commitment" %}

We aim to provide an inclusive environment in which everyone has the opportunity to participate in and enjoy hockey, whether as a player, coach, umpire, official, volunteer, parent, supporter or spectator.

The Club will:

  • welcome and respect people from all backgrounds
  • provide fair and appropriate opportunities to participate in Club activities
  • make reasonable efforts to identify and remove barriers to participation
  • promote a culture in which differences are respected and valued
  • make reasonable adjustments to enable disabled people to participate where practicable
  • challenge discrimination, harassment, bullying and other behaviour which undermines an inclusive Club environment

Decisions about membership, participation, selection, coaching and volunteering will be made fairly and on appropriate grounds, taking account of the requirements of hockey and relevant England Hockey rules and policies.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Discrimination and Harassment" %}

The Club does not tolerate discrimination, harassment or victimisation on the grounds of age, disability, gender reassignment (including trans people), marriage or civil partnership, pregnancy or maternity, race, religion or belief, sex or sexual orientation, or on the basis of other personal characteristics or background.

These expectations apply to behaviour on and off the pitch, including training, matches, social activities and Club-related communications and online activity.

Members are expected to follow the Club's Behaviour Policy and Code of Conduct and England Hockey's Spirit of Hockey – Code of Conduct.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Participation and England Hockey Policies" %}

Participation in hockey is subject to the rules and regulations of England Hockey and the relevant competitions in which the Club participates.

The Club follows England Hockey's current policies and guidance concerning equality and participation, including:

  • Equality Policy
  • Pregnancy Policy and Guidance
  • Trans and Non-Binary Participation Policy and Guidance

Where an individual participation or eligibility question requires specialist guidance, the Club will seek advice from England Hockey.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Raising a Concern" %}

Anyone who experiences or witnesses discrimination, harassment or other behaviour inconsistent with this policy is encouraged to raise it with a Club officer, captain, coach or Welfare Officer.

Concerns will be taken seriously and dealt with under the appropriate Club or England Hockey procedure. No member should be disadvantaged for raising a genuine concern or supporting someone else who does so.
