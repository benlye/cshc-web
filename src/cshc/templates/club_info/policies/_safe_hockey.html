{% include 'blocks/_middle_heading.html' with title="Safe Hockey" %}

Cambridge South Hockey Club is committed to providing a safe environment in which everyone can enjoy hockey.

Safety is a shared responsibility. The Club, coaches, captains, officials and players all have a role in identifying and managing risks and in taking reasonable care of themselves and others.

The Club follows England Hockey's Planning Safe Hockey guidance and other current England Hockey safety policies and guidance.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Safe Hockey Activities" %}

The Club will take reasonable steps to ensure that matches, training sessions and other hockey activities are planned and delivered safely.

This includes:

  • assessing and managing risks associated with the facilities and activities we use
  • taking appropriate action where pitches, goals, equipment, weather or other conditions present a safety risk
  • ensuring activities are appropriate for the age, ability and experience of participants
  • providing appropriate first aid arrangements
  • maintaining appropriate emergency procedures
  • reviewing safety arrangements following significant incidents or changes in circumstances

Where the Club uses facilities provided by another organisation, we will work with the facility provider and follow their relevant safety and emergency procedures.

Activities should not proceed where a significant risk has been identified which cannot be adequately managed.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Player Responsibilities" %}

Players are expected to take reasonable care of their own safety and that of others.

Players should:

  • follow the Rules of Hockey and instructions from coaches, umpires and officials
  • use equipment appropriately and report damaged or unsafe equipment
  • report hazards, injuries or other safety concerns promptly
  • not participate where injury, illness or another condition means that doing so would present an unreasonable risk to themselves or others
  • use appropriate protective equipment
{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Protective Equipment" %}

Players should use appropriate protective equipment during training and matches.

In line with England Hockey guidance, the Club strongly recommends that all players wear appropriate shin protection and a mouthguard during training and matches.

For players under 18, appropriate shin protection and a mouthguard are mandatory during training and matches, and players will not be permitted to participate without this protection.

The Club also strongly recommends the use of appropriate face protection when defending penalty corners. The Club provides face masks for this purpose, although players may use their own suitable face protection if they prefer.

Players are responsible for ensuring that any personal protective equipment they use is suitable, correctly fitted and in good condition. Club-provided face masks should be used appropriately and any damage or defects reported promptly.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="First Aid and Emergencies" %}

The Club will assess the first aid requirements of its hockey activities and will aim to ensure that appropriate first aid provision is available at matches and training sessions.

In accordance with England Hockey guidance, the Club aims to have at least one appropriately trained first aider available at each match or training session.

Appropriate first aid equipment should be readily available and coaches, captains and team officials should know how to obtain emergency assistance at the facilities they use.

Where an injury or illness requires urgent medical attention, obtaining appropriate medical assistance takes priority. Emergency treatment and the use of medical information are covered by the Club's Medical and Emergency Treatment Policy.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Injuries and Injury Reporting" %}

Injuries should be treated appropriately and players should not be encouraged to continue participating where doing so may make an injury worse or place them or others at risk.

The Club will record and report injuries in accordance with England Hockey's current Injury Reporting Policy.

In particular, England Hockey requires injuries to be reported where a player:

  • receives treatment from a first aid specialist or first responder
  • requires hospital treatment
  • subsequently visits a GP as a result of the injury
  • sustains a suspected or confirmed concussion or other significant head injury

Where there is doubt about whether an injury should be reported, it should be reported.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Concussion and Head Injuries" %}

The Club follows England Hockey and UK grassroots sport concussion guidance.

Any player with a suspected concussion must be removed from play immediately and must not return to the activity that day:

If in doubt, sit them out.

Players with suspected or confirmed concussion must follow the current England Hockey and UK grassroots sport guidance for recovery and return to hockey.

Coaches, captains and players must not encourage or permit somebody with a suspected concussion to continue playing simply because they say that they feel able to do so.

Suspected or confirmed concussion and other significant head injuries must be reported to England Hockey.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Raising Safety Concerns" %}

Everyone involved in Club activities is encouraged to identify and report hazards or unsafe practices.

Immediate safety concerns should be raised with the coach, captain, umpire or other person responsible for the activity so that appropriate action can be taken.

More general concerns about health and safety should be raised with a Club officer.

The Club will review significant incidents and safety concerns and make changes where reasonably necessary to reduce the risk of recurrence.
