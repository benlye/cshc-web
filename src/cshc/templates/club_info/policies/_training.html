Club training is intended to help our players develop their skills, fitness and understanding of the game, while building effective teams and an enjoyable and supportive club environment.

Players are encouraged to attend training regularly and to take an active part in sessions appropriate to their team or playing level.

Training sessions are planned and led by the Club's coaches, who may organise players into different groups according to ability, position, development needs or the objectives of the session.

Where training is organised for specific squads or groups of teams, attendance is by invitation. Players should attend the session to which they have been allocated and should not attend a higher squad's training session unless invited to do so by the relevant coach or captain.

Players are expected to:

  • arrive ready to train at the advertised start time
  • participate positively and give their best effort
  • follow the instructions of coaches
  • treat coaches and other players with respect
  • train safely and with consideration for others

Regular training is an important part of individual development and team cohesion. Attendance, engagement and performance at training may therefore be taken into account when teams are selected, in accordance with the Club's Selection Policy.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Attendance" %}

We recognise that work, study, family commitments, injury and other circumstances may prevent players from attending every session. Players should let their captain or coach know when they are unable to train, particularly where sessions are organised around specific squads.

Players who are injured should not take part where doing so could put themselves or others at risk, but are encouraged to discuss with their coach whether they can participate in an appropriate modified session.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Guests and Players from Other Clubs" %}

Prospective members are welcome to attend an appropriate training session by prior arrangement with the Club.

Players who are registered with, or regularly play for, another club whose teams compete against the Club in the same league structure may not normally attend our training sessions.

This restriction does not normally apply to players representing a university, school, representative or other side which does not compete against the Club in the same league structure.

Exceptions may be agreed in advance by the relevant Club Captain or coach, for example where a player is considering joining the Club or where there are other exceptional circumstances.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Safety and Conduct" %}

All players must follow the instructions of the coach responsible for the session and comply with the Club's codes of conduct and relevant England Hockey safety guidance.

Players must use appropriate protective equipment and must not participate in training where their condition, behaviour or equipment presents an unreasonable risk to themselves or others.
