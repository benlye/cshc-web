{% include 'blocks/_middle_heading.html' with title="Data and Privacy" %}

The Club collects and uses personal information in order to administer the Club, organise hockey and other activities, communicate with members, and maintain records of participation and playing history.

We will only collect and use information that we reasonably need, will take appropriate steps to keep it secure, and will not sell members' personal information or use it for unrelated purposes.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Information We Hold" %}

The information we hold may include:

  • name and contact details
  • date of birth and other information required for player registration and eligibility
  • emergency contact details
  • membership, team and availability information
  • medical information relevant to participation in hockey
  • records relating to matches, training and other Club activities
  • playing records and statistics
  • other information provided by members where it is reasonably required for the operation of the Club

Access to non-public personal information is restricted according to role and to those who require the information for a legitimate Club purpose.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="How We Use Your Information" %}

Personal information may be used to:

  • administer membership and subscriptions
  • organise teams, training, matches and other Club activities
  • communicate with members
  • manage player availability and selection
  • support player safety, safeguarding and welfare
  • administer competitions and comply with England Hockey and league requirements
  • maintain the Club's playing records, statistics and history

We process personal information where it is necessary to administer membership and Club activities, to meet our legal or regulatory obligations, where we have a legitimate interest in doing so, or where consent has been provided.

Where we rely on consent, that consent may be withdrawn at any time.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Medical Information" %}

The Club may ask members to provide details of medical conditions which are relevant to their safe participation in hockey, such as asthma, allergies or other conditions which coaches or team officials may need to be aware of.

Providing this information is optional. Where a member chooses to provide medical information, the Club will ask for their explicit consent, or appropriate consent from a parent or carer where applicable, to hold and use that information for the purposes of player safety and welfare.

Medical information is treated as confidential and access is restricted to Club officers, coaches, captains or other authorised volunteers where they have an appropriate reason to know it. It will not be published or used for unrelated purposes.

Where necessary for a player's safety or welfare, relevant information may be shared with medical professionals, emergency services or others providing assistance.

Members should ensure that medical information provided to the Club remains accurate and up to date.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Club Website and Playing Records" %}

The Club maintains its own website and database containing membership and playing information.

Personal contact details, medical information and other private membership information are not made publicly available.

Playing information such as team selections, appearances, results, goals, cards, awards and other hockey statistics may be published on the Club website and retained as part of the Club's historical playing records.

Historical playing records may continue to be retained and published after a player ceases to be a member of the Club.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Spond" %}

The Club uses Spond to help organise teams, training, matches, events and communications.

Information required for these purposes may be stored and processed using Spond. Members using Spond are also subject to Spond's own privacy policy and terms of service.

Access to Club groups within Spond is restricted to members and others who have an appropriate reason to participate.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="England Hockey GMS" %}

Players participating in competitions administered through England Hockey's Game Management System (GMS) are required to register with GMS

GMS is operated by England Hockey and maintains player profiles, registrations, match records and playing history. The Club's authorised administrators and team officials may access and update information in GMS where required to administer teams and competitions.

Information held directly by England Hockey is subject to England Hockey's own privacy policy and terms of use.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Keeping Information Secure" %}

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

Access to Club systems and personal information is limited according to role. Club officers, captains, coaches and volunteers with access to members' information must use it only for legitimate Club purposes and take appropriate care to keep it secure.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="How Long We Keep Information" %}

We keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably required for the purpose for which it was collected, or where we have a legal, regulatory or safeguarding reason to retain it.

Membership and administrative information which is no longer required will be deleted or anonymised when appropriate.

Playing records, match results and statistics may be retained indefinitely as part of the historical record of the Club.

{% include 'blocks/_middle_subheading.html' with title="Your Information and Your Rights" %}

Members should keep their information up to date and can ask the Club to correct information which is inaccurate.

You may ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you and, where applicable, ask us to correct, delete or restrict the use of your information, object to its processing, or withdraw consent previously provided.

Some information may need to be retained where there is a continuing legal, safeguarding, competition or legitimate Club reason for doing so.

Questions or requests relating to personal information should be directed to the {% clubinfo_email 'SecretaryEmail' secretary_name %}.
